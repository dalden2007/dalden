3D Printed Models

A collection of my 3D printed designs that aren't part of my main projects.

Paper Towel Holder

My paper towel was taking too much space on my desk. So I made a printable version that clamps onto the side of my desk. The design doesn't require any tools and are threaded themselves for easy installation.

Bike Phone Holder

Didn't want to buy one from Amazon so I made my own. I used PETG since it was the best weatherproof material I had. I had a couple bike rides with it and it worked pretty well.

CD Shelf

I was starting to collect CDs and I wanted to shelf them neatly. It can carry 8 CDs. I printed using PETG and cut a 5/8" wooden dowel as the pillars.
